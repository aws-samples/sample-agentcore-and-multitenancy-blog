## Clinical Outcomes

**Primary Endpoint - Mobilization Time:**
- Intervention Group (n=23): 14.2 ± 3.1 hours post-op
- Control Group (n=22): 28.6 ± 5.4 hours post-op
- p-value: <0.001

**Secondary Endpoints:**

*Pain Scores (VAS Scale at 48 hours):*
- Intervention: 3.8 ± 1.2
- Control: 5.4 ± 1.8

*Length of Stay:*
- Intervention: 3.2 ± 0.8 days
- Control: 4.9 ± 1.3 days

*Complications:*
- DVT occurrence: 2 cases (Control group)
- Wound infections: 1 case (Intervention group)
- No pulmonary embolism events reported

**Functional Recovery (6-week follow-up):**
- Modified Harris Hip Score (THA): 82.4 vs 74.1 (p=0.02)
- Knee Society Score (TKA): 78.9 vs 71.3 (p=0.03)
- Oswestry Disability Index (Spinal): 28.2 vs 35.7 (p=0.04)

---

**Conclusion:** Early mobilization protocol demonstrated statistically significant improvements in time to ambulation, reduced hospital length of stay, and enhanced functional outcomes without increasing adverse events. Data supports implementation across orthopedic service line.

**Adverse Events:** All SAEs reported to IRB within 24 hours per protocol requirements.
