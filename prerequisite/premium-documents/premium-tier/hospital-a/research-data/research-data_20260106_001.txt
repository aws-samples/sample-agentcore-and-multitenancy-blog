# HOSPITAL A MULTI-SPECIALTY MEDICAL CENTER

## Clinical Research Data Summary

**Study Title:** Comparative Analysis of Post-Operative Recovery Protocols in Elective Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy Patients

**Study Period:** January 2024 - March 2024  
**Principal Investigator:** Dr. Sarah Mitchell, MD, FACS  
**IRB Approval Number:** HAMSMC-2024-0847

---

### Patient Cohort Demographics (N=156)

**Intervention Group (Enhanced Recovery Protocol):** n=78
- Mean Age: 52.3 years (SD ±12.4)
- Gender Distribution: 64% Female, 36% Male
- Mean BMI: 28.7 kg/m² (SD ±4.2)

**Control Group (Standard Protocol):** n=78
- Mean Age: 51.8 years (SD ±11.9)
- Gender Distribution: 62% Female, 38% Male
- Mean BMI: 29.1 kg/m² (SD ±4.5)

---

### Primary Outcomes

**Length of Hospital Stay:**
- Intervention Group: 18.4 hours (SD ±6.2)
- Control Group: 26.7 hours (SD ±8.9)
- p-value: 0.003

**Post-Operative Pain Scores (VAS at 24 hours):**
- Intervention Group: 3.2/10 (SD ±1.4)
- Control Group: 4.8/10 (SD ±1.9)
- p-value: 0.012

**Return to Normal Activities:**
- Intervention Group: 8.3 days (SD ±2.1)
- Control Group: 11.6 days (SD ±3.4)
- p-value: 0.001

---

### Secondary Outcomes

**Complication Rates:**
- Intervention: 5.1% (4 cases: 2 wound infections, 2 nausea/vomiting)
- Control: 7.7% (6 cases: 3 wound infections, 2 bile leaks, 1 urinary retention)

**Readmission Rate (30-day):**
- Intervention: 2.6% (2 patients)
- Control: 5.1% (4 patients)

---

### Conclusion

The enhanced recovery after surgery (ERAS) protocol demonstrated statistically significant improvements in post-operative outcomes including reduced hospital stay, lower pain scores, and faster return to baseline function compared to standard care protocols.
