**RESEARCH PROTOCOL DATA SUMMARY**

**Study Title:** Efficacy of Novel Anticoagulation Protocol in Post-Operative Cardiac Surgery Patients

**Protocol ID:** HBAMC-2024-CR-087  
**Principal Investigator:** Dr. Michelle Torres, MD, FACC  
**Study Period:** January 2024 - March 2024

---

**PATIENT COHORT DEMOGRAPHICS (N=45)**

Mean Age: 64.3 years (SD ±8.7)  
Gender Distribution: 27 Male (60%), 18 Female (40%)  
Mean BMI: 28.4 kg/m² (SD ±4.2)

**PRIMARY OUTCOME MEASURES:**

*Treatment Group (n=23):*
- Mean INR at 72 hours: 2.1 (Range: 1.8-2.6)
- Thromboembolic events: 1 (4.3%)
- Major bleeding complications: 0 (0%)
- Mean length of stay: 6.2 days

*Control Group (n=22):*
- Mean INR at 72 hours: 2.4 (Range: 1.6-3.8)
- Thromboembolic events: 2 (9.1%)
- Major bleeding complications: 3 (13.6%)
- Mean length of stay: 8.1 days

**LABORATORY VALUES (Mean at Discharge):**

Treatment Group:
- Hemoglobin: 11.2 g/dL
- Platelet count: 198 × 10⁹/L
- aPTT: 34.2 seconds
- D-dimer: 850 ng/mL

Control Group:
- Hemoglobin: 10.4 g/dL
- Platelet count: 175 × 10⁹/L
- aPTT: 42.8 seconds
- D-dimer: 1,240 ng/mL

**ADVERSE EVENTS:**

Minor hematoma formation: 4 cases (treatment), 7 cases (control)  
Transfusion requirement: 2 units (treatment), 8 units (control)

**STATISTICAL ANALYSIS:**

P-value for primary endpoint: 0.042 (significant)  
95% CI for risk reduction: 1.2-8.7%

**CONCLUSION:**

Preliminary data demonstrates improved anticoagulation stability and reduced bleeding complications with the novel protocol. Study continuation recommended for Phase III trial expansion.
