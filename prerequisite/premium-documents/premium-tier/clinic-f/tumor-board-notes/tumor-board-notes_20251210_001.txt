**CASE SUMMARY:**

Patient presents with newly diagnosed stage IIIB adenocarcinoma of the right upper lobe. Initial presentation included persistent cough, hemoptysis, and 15-pound weight loss over three months. Non-smoker with no significant family history of malignancy.

**DIAGNOSTIC FINDINGS:**

*Imaging:* PET-CT scan reveals 4.2 cm spiculated mass in RUL with SUV max 8.7. Mediastinal lymphadenopathy involving stations 2R, 4R, and 7. No evidence of distant metastasis.

*Pathology:* Core needle biopsy confirmed moderately differentiated adenocarcinoma. Immunohistochemistry positive for TTF-1 and napsin A. 

*Molecular Testing:* EGFR wild-type, ALK negative, PD-L1 expression 65%.

*Pulmonary Function:* FEV1 72% predicted, adequate for surgical consideration pending neoadjuvant therapy.

---

**TUMOR BOARD RECOMMENDATIONS:**

1. **Primary Treatment:** Concurrent chemoradiation with carboplatin/pemetrexed and 60 Gy radiation therapy over 6 weeks
2. **Immunotherapy:** Add durvalumab consolidation therapy post-chemoradiation given high PD-L1 expression
3. **Surgical Re-evaluation:** Assess for surgical resectability after neoadjuvant therapy completion
4. **Supportive Care:** Refer to palliative care for symptom management and nutritional support

**Follow-up:** Repeat imaging in 8 weeks. Return to tumor board for surgical candidacy discussion.
